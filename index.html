About The Author

Warren Riad AYITOU

Architect, author and visionary thinker, Warren Riad AYITOU develops an architectural language rooted in clarity, human presence and intellectual depth.

His work approaches design as a disciplined act of listening: to context, to material, to silence, and to the ways people inhabit the world.
